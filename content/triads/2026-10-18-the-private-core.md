## The thread

Friel's Translations (1980) borrows close to verbatim from Steiner's After Babel (1975): 'signals and conventionalized counters', the 'linguistic contour' that no longer matches 'the changing landscape of fact', cultures that 'expend on their vocabulary and syntax acquisitive energies and ostentations', the 'private core', the 'pass-word', 'hermetic', even Jimmy's exogamein against Steiner's 'linguistic exogamy'. Ó hAodha's 'Insubordinate Irish' (2011) reports that Shelta, the Travellers' Cant/Gammon, was probably 'formed in the modern period, possibly at that juncture when the language shift from Irish to English was taking place', the same shift the Name-Book stages: Irish words hidden inside English grammar by metathesis, syllable reversal and prefixes. Steiner makes concealment the genius of language; the Gypsilorists read Shelta as 'of the backslang order' and valued it only if ancient, the same romance of antiquity that has Yolland admiring 'Termon, from Terminus'.

- [[books/2372-after-babel-aspects-of-language-and-translation|After Babel: Aspects of Language and Translation]], [[people/george-steiner|George Steiner]]; 1975. *Source: the theory Friel borrowed close to verbatim (genealogy edge)*
- [[books/13399-insubordinate-irish-travellers-in-the-text|'Insubordinate Irish': Travellers in the Text]], [[people/micheal-o-haodha|Mícheál Ó hAodha]]; 2011. *Oblique edge: Shelta as a living private core, likely formed in the same Irish-to-English shift; its outside 'discoverers' as the philologist's counterpart to the sappers*

## Notebook

1. The ledger exercise: from the three Lancey/Owen pairs (Act One), write a fourth pair for a document from your own present, with an 'Owen' column for the people affected. What does Owen's softening protect, and whom? Compare Steiner's 'safeguard of obliqueness' (Ch. 3).
2. Restitution, missing: read Steiner's four beats (trust–aggression–incorporation–restitution, 1992 preface) as the plot of Translations; write the withheld fourth beat as one scene or page. If it won't work, record where it breaks.
3. Gammon and the Gesturalist: using Meyer's devices (metathesis, apocope, syllable reversal, prefixes; Ó hAodha p. 20), draft a cant for one of your concepts (gesture, utterance). Is it a private core in Steiner's sense or only 'backslang'? What's the difference?
