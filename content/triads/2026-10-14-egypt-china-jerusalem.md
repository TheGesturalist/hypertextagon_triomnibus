## The thread

Three positions inside one building type. Yanni (The Architecture of Madness) is the plan: Kirkbride's shallow-V linear plan graded patients ward by ward, 'with the noisiest patients farthest away from the center' (p. 60), and 'a transfer to a nicer ward was a reward for a well-behaved patient' (p. 64). Machado (The Alienist) is the theory pushed to its end: the Green House's boundary is redrawn until four-fifths of Itaguai is inside, then the well balanced are sorted into galleries by virtue, then Bacamarte commits himself. Carrington (Down Below) is the inmate who reads Dr. Morales's pavilions as Egypt (Covadonga), China (Amachu) and Jerusalem (Down Below, 'very luxurious, like a hotel'): her delusion gets the ward-as-reward structure right in the wrong vocabulary ('you're not well enough to go there'). Same subject + oblique/structural + conflict (plan vs patient, theory vs town).
