| Method | Status | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| Triads | Volume 1 (24 Sep – 25 Oct 2026); on hand. Instructions: `method/triads_prompt_v1.md` in the repository | Three books sharing a precisely nameable connection, read from their own files; quotations verified. |
| An author and their shadow | Active from the first run of the new instructions; first author Elias Canetti. Draft instructions: `method/shadow_prompt_v0.2.md` | One author per week: day one the author's own work; then, via the library's full-text index, who quotes, argues with, translates, or uses them without credit. Produces a map of citation inside the library. |
| Lens and specimen | On hand | A week with one book about reading; each day one of its procedures applied to a single book. |
| News oracle | On hand | One concrete phrase from the previous day's news, searched across the library; the book chosen is the one that illuminates it from the greatest distance. |
| Margin and Lexicon | On hand | Start from a highlight or a Lexicon term and trace its occurrences through the library with verified quotations. |
| Mnemosyne panel | On hand | Five to nine short passages sharing a gesture, arranged without a stated thesis; deformance as a variant. |

The authoritative list lives in the `Methods` table of the Grist document *Library Resurfacing*; every daily set records which method produced it.
