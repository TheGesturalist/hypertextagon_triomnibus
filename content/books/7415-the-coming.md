Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-22-the-day-after-his-arrival|The Day After His Arrival]] (2026-10-22).

## Role in the triad

The novelist's case: a noir in Ulcinj where Sabbatai's followers burn books so he can return; the non-occurrence of the End as the catastrophe.

## Why this book

Fiction set in Sabbatai's exile town that can be checked line by line against Scholem (birth, exile, death date, last words); Kafka epigraph and Dr Schulz's catalogue of failed Ends. Montenegrin Dolazak 2009; English © 2011 (Dzanc).
