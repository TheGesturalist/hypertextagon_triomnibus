Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-18-the-private-core|The Private Core]] (2026-10-18).

## Role in the triad

Oblique edge: Shelta as a living private core, likely formed in the same Irish-to-English shift; its outside 'discoverers' as the philologist's counterpart to the sappers

## Why this book

Gives the Victorian origin myths of Shelta and the modern view that it was formed during the language shift; shows outsiders valuing a hidden tongue only for antiquity and reading concealment as degeneracy.
