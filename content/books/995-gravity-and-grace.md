## Role in the triad

Anchor / source of the word: attention defined against the will, the will confined to the muscles

## Why this book

Both other books answer ch. 26 'Attention and Will': Murdoch borrows the word, Polanyi finds attention at work in exactly the muscular region Weil assigns to the will, while agreeing with her on indirection.
