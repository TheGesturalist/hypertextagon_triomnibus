## Role in the triad

Anchor: the graph narrated from inside — a building walked one room per chapter; the Preamble states the relational premise.

## Why this book

The jigsaw Preamble ('the pattern determines the parts') is structuralism in a puzzle-maker's voice; the novel stages it as 99 chapters over 100 squares (knight's tour, unstated in the text) and ends with a piece that cannot fit (W vs X). Its own contents yield the walk: Stairs 12 chapters, 36 locations.
