## Role in the triad

Hinge: quotes Polo against the Physiologus, cites Gombrich, theorizes the negotiated type; supplies the title.

## Why this book

Opens with Marco Polo calling the rhinoceros a unicorn and denying the virgin's-lap legend; tells the platypus's 1797–1884 taxonomic history as 'a long negotiation'; reworks Kant's schematism through Peirce into cognitive types and contractual realism.
