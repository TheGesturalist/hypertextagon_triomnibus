**[[people/sir-thomas-browne|Sir Thomas Browne]]** · 1658 · EPUB · Calibre ID 149

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-20-the-chemists-shelf|The Chemist’s Shelf]] (2026-10-20).

## Role in the triad

Hinge: holds both Russian positions at once — faith that scattered ashes reassemble (God's work) and scepticism toward every human preservation; mummy ends on the apothecary's shelf.

## Why this book

Religio Medici I.47–48 states resurrection of divided dust and supports it by palingenesis (plant revived from its ashes); Urne-Buriall V catalogues the failures of pyramids, mummies, inscriptions; uses 'Ossuaries' (1658). Years: RM 1642/43, UB 1658; NYRB ed. 2012.

## Quotations
