**[[people/vladimir-nabokov|Vladimir Nabokov]], [[people/gennady-barabtarlo|Gennady Barabtarlo]] (editor)** · 1964-65 (cards); 2017 (book) · EPUB · Calibre ID 2515

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-10-the-dated-slip|The Dated Slip]] (2026-10-10).

## Role in the triad

The experiment: dated cards as lab equipment to test Dunne's 'reverse memory'; the second memory surprising its author (1968 note on a 1964 card).

## Why this book

118 Oxford cards used to catch time running backwards; his own dream taxonomy sits on an undated card filed out of order; the log thins, misdates November as October, and ends in a dream of the dead.
