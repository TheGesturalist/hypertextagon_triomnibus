**[[people/stanisaw-lem|Stanisław Lem]], [[people/michael-kandel|Michael Kandel]] (translator)** · 1971 · PDF · Calibre ID 7480

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-17-nothing-reviewed|Nothing, Reviewed]] (2026-10-17).

## Role in the triad

Anchor: the impossible blank book, reviewed; the extreme position (nothing reached only by language's self-annihilation)

## Why this book

The 'Rien du tout, ou la consequence' review is a pure thought experiment: a novel of total negation whose critic admits the reader supplies everything (railway station, erotic encounter). Supplies the review form and the claim that a decent literature must cease rather than end.

## Quotations

*Verified against the book's own text on the day of the reading.*

- "It was necessary, then—and herein is the consequence—to write nothing." (p. 71)
- "Total freedom is total paralysis in literature." (p. 72)
- "Instead of a print we obtain an exact negative, that is all. The nature of an innovation must be ontological, and not simply grammatical!" (p. 74)
- "Nonbeing is already known to us in literature, but only as a certain Lack—of Something—for Someone." (p. 75)
- "How can a thing be in bad taste which is not there at all?!" (p. 76)
- "The novel does not end: it ceases." (p. 79)
- "a critique of a book that not only does not exist but also cannot." (p. 4)
