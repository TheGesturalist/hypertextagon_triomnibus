## Role in the triad

The anchor and the historian's case: three disconfirmations (imprisonment, apostasy, death) each turned into doctrine; explicit parallel with Paul and early Christianity.

## Why this book

The best-documented case of a faith surviving refutation of its central claim; Scholem himself invokes Renan and Paul, which brings in Schweitzer. Duplicate record: 5724. Hebrew 1957; English 1973; Princeton Classics 2016. Pages are print pages embedded in the EPUB.
