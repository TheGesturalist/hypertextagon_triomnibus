Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-26-certificates-without-originals|Certificates Without Originals]] (2026-09-26).

## Role in the triad

The theory both fictions test: the discourse of originality rests on a repressed discourse of the copy.

## Why this book

Names the certifying machinery Zweig's councillor points to ('the mark, the warrant, the certification of the original'), argues spontaneity is manufactured (Winckler's jaw, reversed), and starts from Benjamin on the print, the medium of Zweig's portfolios.
