Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-20-the-chemists-shelf|The Chemist’s Shelf]] (2026-10-20).

## Role in the triad

The resurrection programme: Fedorov removes Browne's deferral to God and gives the work to art/biology; Groys makes the museum the machine of immortality; Bogdanov's 'Immortality Day' ends on a pyre.

## Why this book

Groys's Introduction ('Russian Cosmism and the Technology of Immortality') states the museum-as-immortality thesis Malevich attacks; Fedorov's 'Astronomy and Architecture' (Earth as cemetery, art as resurrection, contempt for portrait-substitutes); Svyatogor demotes resurrection; Bogdanov's immortal Fride chooses the stake. Edition MIT/e-flux 2018; texts c.1880s–1920s.
