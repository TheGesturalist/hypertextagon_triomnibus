## Role in the triad

The source: forehead brushed in the dark, fever, train south, the store like an engraving, the dagger; two readings (events / dream on the operating table).

## Why this book

Supplies the template both later texts inherit and Borges's own instruction that it can be read 'in quite another way, as well'. Its open question ('a bat? a bird?') is answered by Aira; its plot is rewritten by name in Bolaño.
