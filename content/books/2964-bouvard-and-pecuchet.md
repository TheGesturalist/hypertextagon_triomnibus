## Role in the triad

The copy: encyclopedic reading as comedy and disease; the Dictionary of Accepted Ideas as hinge to Mullen.

## Why this book

Burton's negative image. Two amateurs do the same encyclopedic reading, stupidity replaces melancholy as the subject, and keeping busy becomes the novel's engine. The Dalkey edition includes the Dictionary of Accepted Ideas, whose entries (MELANCHOLY, DICTIONARY, BOOK, LATIN) land on the other two books. Tagged priority_tbr.
