## Role in the triad

Ground: learning and teaching without masters, under law that forbade both.

## Why this book

Jacotot's ignorant masters in fact (native schools taught by people who had only just learned to read); the antiliteracy statutes (1819–1834 cluster) fall inside Jacotot's career; the 'self-made' that Steiner names, at its real cost.
