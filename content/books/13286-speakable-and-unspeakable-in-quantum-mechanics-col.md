Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-25-the-two-clocks-problem|The Two Clocks Problem]] (2026-09-25).

## Role in the triad

The oblique edge: a physicist who meets the two-clock structure (egg and alarm clock; option 3, superdeterminism) and refuses it.

## Why this book

Bell's analysis of correlation versus cause restates the occasionalist problem without theology. Casimir's egg-and-alarm passage in 'La nouvelle cuisine' is the two clocks in a kitchen, and Bell's four attitudes after Aspect map what each book in the triad gives up.
