## Role in the triad

Counter-voice: keeps the posture and the word illumination, makes it profane; the protocol with a hole in it

## Why this book

Benjamin lies down and waits with a physician keeping the record, and argues the source of illumination is profane. Fränkel's protocol, broken off when note-taking is forbidden, is the best evidence for the observation problem.

## Quotations

- "But the true, creative overcoming of religious illumination certainly does not lie in narcotics. It resides in a profane illumination, a materialistic, anthropological inspiration" ('Surrealism', p. 132)

- "The reader, the thinker, the person who waits, the flaneur, are types of illuminati—just as much as the opium eater, the dreamer, the ecstatic. And more profane." (p. 134)
