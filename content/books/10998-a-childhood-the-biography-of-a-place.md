## Role in the triad

The view from inside: the child who rewrites the wish-image

## Why this book

Crews, born 1935 into share-farming in Bacon County, Georgia, is the Burroughs children's age. With Willalee Bookatee and Lottie Mae, children of the Black tenant family Agee's report excluded, he turns the Sears 'Wish Book' into feuds and maimings, supplying the scars the catalogue hides: the reverse of the documentary gaze. Never mentions Agee; the bridge is built here.
