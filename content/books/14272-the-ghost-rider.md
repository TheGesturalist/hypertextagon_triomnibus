## Role in the triad

Anchor: the oath as a social institution; a besa kept by a dead man, raised by his mother's curse

## Why this book

The only fiction of the three that treats the oath as an institution rather than a plot device: the besa is debated like a constitution (ch. 6) and given a resurrection (ch. 7). Captain Stres's search for a living impostor gives the triad its rationalist resistance. Bellos's introduction ties the ballad to Bürger's 'Lenore'.
