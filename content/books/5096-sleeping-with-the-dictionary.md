## Role in the triad

The dictionary: the reference work as partner rather than pathology; the emptied cliché.

## Why this book

Takes the reference book back from Flaubert's pathology. Mullen writes with the dictionary and thesaurus as collaborators, in alphabetical order. In 'Elliptical' she empties Flaubert's stock-phrase device and keeps only the frame. Like Burton's, hers is night-work.
