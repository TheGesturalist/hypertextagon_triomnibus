## Role in the triad

The return: the subjects read their own picture

## Why this book

Revisits Agee's three families in the mid-1980s under his pseudonyms and records the book coming home: Junior's fury, Fred Ricketts's pleasure, Maggie Louise (Agee's Lucile) reading her mother's copy, Emma's retort. Opens a section on a 1941 USDA schoolbook, 'Round the World with Cotton, dug out of an abandoned school, matching Agee's opening. Answers Crews from the other side.
