## Role in the triad

The modern case where the buyer is named: Perloff's afterword on the American appetite for an 'authentic other'

## Why this book

Collects the notebooks, the editors' apparatus, Johnson's defence (Pessoa, Pushkin, Kierkegaard) and Perloff's catalogue of tells; the Barthes footnote shows an alibi that fixes one anachronism and exposes another. ~23% of notebook-section words are bracketed apparatus.
