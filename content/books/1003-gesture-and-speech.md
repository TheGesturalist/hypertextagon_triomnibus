## Role in the triad

The source: graphism begins as rhythmic, abstract incision; writing linearizes it into a 'needle-sharp, but also needle-thin' line; the Jacquard loom exteriorizes the program.

## Why this book

Ingold builds the close of his writing chapter on it; supplies the triad's deepest claim (abstraction and the rhythmic incised line come first). Printed page = PDF page − 20 in the 'Language Symbols' chapters.
