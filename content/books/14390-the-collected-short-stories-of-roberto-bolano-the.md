**[[people/roberto-bolano|Roberto Bolaño]], [[people/chris-andrews|Chris Andrews]] (translator)** · 2003 (El gaucho insufrible); Eng. 2010 · EPUB · Calibre ID 14390

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-02-something-brushed-his-forehead|Something Brushed His Forehead]] (2026-10-02).

## Role in the triad

The declared descendant: 'The South' rewritten in the 2001 crisis as farce and entropy; rabbits for horses and cattle; the knife fight moved back to Buenos Aires.

## Why this book

Cites Borges and 'The South' by name inside the story; every element of Borges's plot returns transformed. Bolaño also wrote the preface to the Aira volume in the set, so he links both edges.
