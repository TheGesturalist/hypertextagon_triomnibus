## Role in the triad

The counter-position and its institutional sequel: Malevich's 'On the Museum' (1919) — the dead as powder on one chemist's shelf — and Levitsky's 1931 review of a crematorium exhibition.

## Why this book

Prints Fedorov's 'The Museum, its Meaning and Mission' (Part I) beside Malevich (Part II) and the Atheists' Museum section (Part VI); word-frequency by part shows resurrect-/dead vanish after Part I and cremat- peaks in Part VI. Printed page = PDF page − 1.
