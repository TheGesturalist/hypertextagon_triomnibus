**[[people/georges-perec|Georges Perec]], [[people/david-bellos|David Bellos]] (translator)** · written 1957–60; French publ. 2012 (Le Condottière); Eng. 2014 · EPUB · Calibre ID 782

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-26-certificates-without-originals|Certificates Without Originals]] (2026-09-26).

## Role in the triad

The object without its provenance: a forger with certificates for everything who cannot make an authentic Antonello, and finds his own face instead.

## Why this book

Inverts Zweig: certification is trivial ('They had them'), authenticity impossible. Winckler believes in the original enough to kill over it, which stages the conflict with Krauss from inside fiction. Antonello's cartellino ('me pinxit') is the self-certifying mark he cannot forge.
