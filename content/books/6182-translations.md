Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-18-the-private-core|The Private Core]] (2026-10-18).

## Role in the triad

Anchor: the scene the other two books theorize (1833 Name-Book; a closed speech-community opened by translation)

## Why this book

A play about translation written entirely in English; dramatizes the Ordnance Survey's Anglicising of Irish place names and voices Steiner's theory of the private core through Hugh and Yolland.
