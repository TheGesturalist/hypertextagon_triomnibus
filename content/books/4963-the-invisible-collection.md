Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-26-certificates-without-originals|Certificates Without Originals]] (2026-09-26).

## Role in the triad

Anchor: certification without an object. A blind collector shows blank sheets and points to collectors' stamps on their empty versos.

## Why this book

The purest case in the library of provenance outliving the work. It supplies the collector's marks (stamps, states, proofs, 'the Dresden copy') and asks what a connoisseur's description does when there is nothing left to describe. Its treasures are prints, already multiples, which is Krauss's and Benjamin's ground.
