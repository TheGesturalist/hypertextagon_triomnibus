**[[people/francois-jullien|François Jullien]], [[people/jody-gladding|Jody Gladding]] (translator)** · 2012 (Entrer dans une pensée); Eng. 2015 · EPUB · Calibre ID 6355

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-30-only-the-incipit|Only the Incipit]] (2026-09-30).

## Role in the triad

The first sentence as fate: Genesis (creation), Hesiod (generation), Yijing (process); a beginning that 'opens no account'.

## Why this book

Gives the triad its stakes ('We never exit a first sentence'), contests Calvino's equation of incipit with story through the Yijing opening, and supplies the two Western openings Kuppner parodies. Note: EPUB drops the Chinese characters; romanizations only. Same translation also held as PDF, ID 11062.
