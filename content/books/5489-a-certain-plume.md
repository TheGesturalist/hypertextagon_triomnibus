**[[people/henri-michaux|Henri Michaux]], [[people/richard-sieburth|Richard Sieburth]] (translator)** · 1930 · EPUB · Calibre ID 5489

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-27-the-averaged-self|The Averaged Self]] (2026-09-27).

## Role in the triad

The machine run backwards: a particular who never becomes universal; the name thins into initials and the author declares the self an averaged persona.

## Why this book

The indefinite title stages the particular/universal problem; plume = pen, the writing tool personified, which then took over its author ('Votre Plume'); the 1938 Postface ('An averaged "self," a crowd movement') inverts Breen's persona machine.
