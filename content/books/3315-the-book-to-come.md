**[[people/maurice-blanchot|Maurice Blanchot]], [[people/charlotte-mandell|Charlotte Mandell]] (translator)** · 1959 · PDF · Calibre ID 3315

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-13-a-crowd-still-to-come|A Crowd Still to Come]] (2026-10-13).

## Role in the triad

The myth and its class structure: Ulysses at the mast, the oarsmen who watch

## Why this book

'The Song of the Sirens' supplies the song 'only still to come', Ulysses' privileged hearing, the oarsmen's right only to watch, and the Sirens 'conquered by the power of the technique'; the closing essay on Mallarmé's Livre makes the reader an 'operator'. Source of the Mallarmé Rancière politicizes.
