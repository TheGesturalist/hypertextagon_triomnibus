## Role in the triad

Anchor: the case for the ignorant master and the equality of intelligences.

## Why this book

Strongest modern argument that one can teach what one does not know; its target, the explicator, is Steiner's Master; its examples (illiterate father, Lord's Prayer, pupil teaching teacher) are what Williams documents, criminalized, in the same decades.
