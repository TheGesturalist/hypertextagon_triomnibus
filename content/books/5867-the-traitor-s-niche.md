## Role in the triad

Anchor: the state's head on display, and the courier who sells it to villages as their 'literature, theater, art'.

## Why this book

Gestext book from the random pool; the most concrete version of the motif (head on a copper dish in honey, Regulations for the Care of Heads, a keeper, an inscription, an obelisk) and the only book that follows the head's audience out to the frontier, where heads become entertainment and then a calendar.
