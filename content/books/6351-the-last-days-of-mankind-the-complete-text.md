**[[people/karl-kraus|Karl Kraus]], [[people/fred-bridgham|Fred Bridgham]] (translator), [[people/edward-timms|Edward Timms]] (translator)** · 1922 · EPUB · Calibre ID 6351

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-19-except-in-sequence-repetition-omission|Except in Sequence, Repetition, Omission]] (2026-10-19).

## Role in the triad

Ancestor: the documentary method with a commentator (the Grumbler); source of the participle Bäcker's note cites.

## Why this book

First large work built on the claim that ordered quotations accuse their speakers; its Preface disclaims invention; its Przemysl pair shows composition by reversal alone; the Grumbler is the voice Bäcker refuses.

## Quotations
