## Role in the triad

Anchor: the sacred, literal sense of incubation and the supervisor figure

## Why this book

Supplies the older sense of 'incubation' (lying down in a lair, waiting without effort) and the priest 'in charge of the place'; without it Wallas's word reads as neutral metaphor.
