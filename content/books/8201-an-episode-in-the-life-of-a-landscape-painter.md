## Why this book

Its last pages answer 'a bat? a bird?' with 'A bat', the only signature of an undeclared debt. Bolaño's preface opens the edition, which ties it to the third book. Takes the triad outside the recent theory-heavy sets into historical fiction and the history of scientific illustration.
