**[[people/ryszard-kapuscinski|Ryszard Kapuściński]], [[people/klara-glowczewska|Klara Glowczewska]] (translator)** · 2004 (Podróże z Herodotem); trans. 2007 · EPUB · Calibre ID 4197

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-11-only-that-which-is-said|Only That Which Is Said]] (2026-10-11).

## Role in the triad

The anchor: the founding scene of reportage and the hedge as method

## Why this book

Treats Herodotus as the first reporter, whose only archive is living memory; ch. 18 catalogues his distancing formulas, a graded scale of confidence. Kapuściński's own reliability was later questioned by Domosławski, so the problem returns to him.
