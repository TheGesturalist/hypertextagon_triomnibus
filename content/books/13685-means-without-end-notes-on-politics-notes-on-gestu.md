## Role in the triad

The history and the definition: the bourgeoisie lost its gestures; cinema reclaims and records the loss; gesture as gerere, pure means, gag.

## Why this book

Moves Kleist's loss from soul to history (Tourette, Muybridge, Marey, Warburg), explains why Remainder's model is a film actor, and gives the conflict its third term: gesture neither as product nor as end but as means displayed. Its 'gag' (an actor's improvisation for lost memory) names the liver lady's line. OCR text layer; quotes are passages the OCR got right; printed pages (PDF = printed + 10).
