## Role in the triad

The rulebook: the oral historian's ethics, and error as evidence

## Why this book

Introduction states the rules the other two books bend or break (no words put in mouths; cutting-room liberties; translate rather than transcribe) and says plainly that belief and forgetting are evidence. Title is the Herodotean 'they say', from Florence Reece's song.

## Quotations
