**[[people/margaret-cavendish|Margaret Cavendish]]** · 1666 (2nd ed. 1668); file is a 15-page bilingual éditions Clinamen extract · PDF · Calibre ID 349

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-29-the-flea-under-glass|The Flea Under Glass]] (2026-09-29).

## Role in the triad

The philosopher's refusal: the glass deludes; natural eye plus regular reason.

## Why this book

'Of Telescopes' argues the case the other two dramatize: sight works only within 'a certain proportion of distance betwixt the Eye and the Object', past which a glass gives pictures, not natures. The flight section adds the shared frame: 'Art cannot give new motions to natural parts'. Note: the file is only a 15-page extract (four sections), not the Cambridge edition the record describes.

- “whether it be onely a delusion of the glasses, I will not dispute” (Of Telescopes, p. 8)
