## Role in the triad

Deep-time anchor and theory: Galen's market, Serapion's pseudepigrapha, Genette's second degree; Smolak's Seneca–Paul chapter as test case

## Why this book

Supplies the market theory of forgery (Galen) and its rebuttal (Lange: ancient attribution as cultural memory, not fraud), plus the Seneca–Paul letters, which anticipate both modern cases: anachronism as tell, translator as alibi.
