Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-30-only-the-incipit|Only the Incipit]] (2026-09-30).

## Role in the triad

The incipit as the whole book: 4,108 first lines of a lost anthology, alphabetized, no poems behind them.

## Why this book

The purest case in the library of Flannery's 'book that is only an incipit'. Alphabet replaces plot; the phantom country Oblivia (91 lines) supplies a ghost literature; two lines pastiche Hesiod and Genesis, Jullien's Western openings. Title = initials of 'A Revised Index Of First Lines Of The Great Anthology'.
