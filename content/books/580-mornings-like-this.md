## Role in the triad

Control case: same method (omission, never addition) producing delight instead of indictment.

## Why this book

Found poems from old manuals, memoirs and textbooks; the Author's Note states the vow ('I never added a word') and the cost ('the original authors’ intentions were usually first to go'), the exact opposite of Bäcker's use.

## Quotations

*Verified against the book's own text on the day of the reading.*

- "By entering a found text as a poem, the poet doubles its context. The original meaning remains intact, but now it swings between two poles." (Author's Note)
- "In the course of composing such found poems, the original authors’ intentions were usually first to go." (Author's Note)
- "Sometimes I dropped extra words; I never added a word." (Author's Note)
- "This is editing at its extreme: writing without composing." (Author's Note)
- "Stop carrying out your intentions." (Signals at Sea, poem 16)
