Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-12-rewarded-for-the-relics|Rewarded for the Relics]] (2026-10-12).

## Role in the triad

The eighteenth-century hinge: the market made visible document by document (Barrett, Burgum, Walpole)

## Why this book

Taylor traces the Rowleyan documents shifting from free invention to pieces 'designed to exploit the particular needs and interests of actual or potential patrons', shows the apparatus (ruled manuscript, glossary from the A entries) as both authentication and tell, and refuses the word 'forger'.
