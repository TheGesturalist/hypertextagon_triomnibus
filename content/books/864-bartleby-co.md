**[[people/enrique-vila-matas|Enrique Vila-Matas]], [[people/jonathan-dunne|Jonathan Dunne]] (translator)** · 2000 (Eng. 2004) · EPUB · Calibre ID 864

Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-10-08-wrote-nothing-today|Wrote Nothing Today]] (2026-10-08).

## Role in the triad

Counter-diagnosis: not-writing as vocation and canon

## Why this book

Turns Flaherty's disease into a catalogue of 'writers of the No' in 86 footnotes to an invisible text; claims Kafka via the rainy Sunday (note 24) and Melville as founder. Quotes Kafka through Spanish, so it can be compared line by line with Kresh. Performs its own block on Salinger (notes 29–31).

## Quotations

*Verified against the book's own text on the day of the reading.*

- 'I never had much luck with women. ... Apart from that, I am happy. Today most of all because, on this day 8 July 1999, I have begun this diary that is also going to be a book of footnotes commenting on an invisible text, which I hope will prove my reliability as a tracker of Bartlebys.' (opening)
- 'the illness, the disease, endemic to contemporary letters, the negative impulse or attraction towards nothingness ...' (opening)

- 'The problem with Salinger is that he had a certain tendency to repeat himself.' (note 31)
