## Role in the triad

The negative case: the hearsay archive as bare claim, and the composite

## Why this book

Joe Gould's 'Oral History' of 'things I heard people say' that Mitchell concludes doesn't exist; Mitchell's own composite Mr. Flood, 'truthful rather than factual'; and Mitchell's confession of his own unwritten novel. Both break Portelli's first rule in opposite directions.

- "Some talk has an obvious meaning and nothing more, he said, and some, often unbeknownst to the talker, has at least one other meaning and sometimes several other meanings lurking around inside its obvious meaning." (Joe Gould's Secret)
