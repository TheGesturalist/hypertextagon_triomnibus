## Role in the triad

Anchor: the visitor's inventory of the wish-images on the tenant walls

## Why this book

Gestext. The rejected Fortune report behind Let Us Now Praise Famous Men, under the families' real names. Opens on Lucile Burroughs's geography reader and records the Peters Shoes calendar girl 'Cherie' with 'Lucile' pencilled under it twice: the printed outside world inside the tenant house, seen by a visitor writing for a glossy magazine. Its chapters follow the reader's 'Food, Shelter, and Clothing'.
