## Role in the triad

Thesis: structure fills the silence; Cook as image of Lono, death as ritual transformation

## Why this book

Ch. 4, 'Captain James Cook; or, The Dying God' (Frazer Lecture 1982), is the structuralist reading Obeyesekere's Apotheosis attacked; it opens with Home's epigraph on the impossibility of certainty, then finds 'the one recurrent certainty'. Lono-heavy, cannibal-light: the mirror image of Cannibal Talk.
