## Role in the triad

Antithesis: god and cannibal as European projections; the quarterdeck proof as dialogical performance

## Why this book

Extends the Apotheosis argument from the god to the cannibal; ch. 2 reads Cook's 1773 quarterdeck experiment (quoting the same journal passage) and the 1779 Kealakekua bundle; ch. 6 is a second round with Sahlins over Fiji. Its 'confusion' answers Sahlins's 'clarity' explicitly.
