## Role in the triad

Source: the theory Friel borrowed close to verbatim (genealogy edge)

## Why this book

Friel drew on it while writing Translations; its thesis that speech matured through concealment ('All developed language has a private core'; the word as 'pass-word') frames Shelta as well. Second edition with 1992 preface.
