## Role in the triad

Opponent: the eloquent defense of mastery and discipleship.

## Why this book

Reads the Meno against Rancière (Socrates makes autodidacts; the slave boy's proof is unsound); names in one sentence that 'Master' carries the stain of slavery and sets American self-made talent against the magisterium.
