Resurfaced in [[triads/2026-09-27-the-averaged-self|The Averaged Self]] (2026-09-27).

## Role in the triad

The machine takes the floor: a personification delivering her own encomium, refusing definition, mocking the universals debate, and finally forgetting who she is.

## Why this book

Breen stops at the 14th century; Folly is what the device does next. A daemonic type who satirizes the realists and nominalists whose quarrel defines her, with a Psychomachia-like household turned into a court.
