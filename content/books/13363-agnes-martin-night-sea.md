## Role in the triad

The test case: a grid of scored, gilded lines ruled with threads from Tawney's loom; the 'visual tremolo' as Ingold's risk; after 1963 Martin linearizes her own practice.

## Why this book

Material evidence for both theories in one painting; its lines fall under all four of Ingold's classes (thread, trace, cut, ghostly). Printed page = PDF page − 1.
