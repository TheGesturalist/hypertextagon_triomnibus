## Role in the triad

Empirical counterweight: real blank works read closely; denies any true blank

## Why this book

Where Lem invents his blank book, Dworkin catalogues real ones (Nudisme, Saroyan, Rauschenberg, Friedman's erased centerfold, silent records). His 'Further Listening' reviews empty records in a consumer-guide voice, Lem's form inverted. Thesis: 'there are no real absences, only replacements'; media are social acts of reading.
