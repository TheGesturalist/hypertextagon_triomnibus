On these shelves plagiarism becomes a question of translation. (2) Smallest unit at every remove: her changes (eighty→sixty, Pip→Peter, badly→poorly, was→were), her quoters' drift, her editors' eye-skip, Armand's dropped full stop, her defenders' errors (Jenny, right wrist). Her method can't be told apart from what copying does anyway. (3) Names and sentences come apart: GE/MDML cut names and keep sentences; BG keeps names (Hawthorne, Genet, Propertius, Shakespeare) and loses sentences; misattributions (McCarthy's Flaubert, Mr Linker's Hamlet) sit where they meet. (4) No dissent in its own voice; objections survive only inside her defenders.
